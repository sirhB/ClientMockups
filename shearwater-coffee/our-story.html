Our story

An encore career, roasted slowly.

Ed Freedman founded Shearwater in 2013, then Fairfield County’s only small-batch organic coffee roaster.

“It's a way of life, not a product line.”Ed Freedman, founder
Timeline

From a couple hundred pounds a month

2013

Ed Freedman opens the roastery in Trumbull as an encore career — Fairfield County's only small-batch organic roaster.

2017

First Shearwater Coffee Bar opens on the Brick Walk, Fairfield.

2019

About 80,000 lb roasted a year, up from a couple hundred pounds a month.

Today

The cafés have closed. The Trumbull roastery & retail counter carries on, Mon–Fri.

What we believe

Renew the Earth, Restore Your Energy.

01

Organic

Grown without synthetic fertilizers, pesticides, herbicides or insecticides, per USDA NOP requirements.

02

Artisanal

Roasted 20 lb at a time, profiled to each origin's peak, air-cooled quickly so it's never bitter.

03

Sustainable

Diedrich IR-12 infrared roaster, repurposed burlap, organic cleaners and zero-emission EV delivery.

The name

Named for a seabird that crosses oceans.

The shearwater is a long-winged seabird whose migrations span thousands of miles — a fitting picture for green coffee making its own journey to the roastery in Connecticut.
