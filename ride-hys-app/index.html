Your black car, booked like a flight.

Hy’s Limousine Worldwide · West Haven, CT · Since 1951

One system, three sides: the rider books in taps, the chauffeur drives the trip, and dispatch watches the whole fleet live. The Ride Hys app, rebuilt around the trip itself.

  1. 1Rider books. Pick your airports, choose the car, see the fare before you confirm.
  2. 2Chauffeur drives. The trip lands on the driver’s phone — accept, navigate, complete, get paid.
  3. 3Dispatch watches. Every car, every trip, every pickup on one live board — no radio, no whiteboard.
Control center
  • No surge pricing

  • On-time pickups

Why rebuild it? The current Ride Hys app sits at 2.0★ (8 ratings) on the App Store, running a generic white-label shell from 2015. Hy’s has spent 75 years earning trust in person — the app should feel like the car, not like a form.

Why rebuild

Same destination. A better way to get there.

The app today

  • 2.0★ from 8 App Store ratings
  • A generic white-label shell, unchanged since 2015
  • Booking still ends in a phone call
  • No fare shown before you commit
  • No live view of your chauffeur

This concept

  • Book in taps — “without making a single phone call”
  • Fare up front, on every car, no surge
  • Live chauffeur, car and plate on the map
  • Trips and receipts kept in one place
  • Hy’s own voice: black, brass, and “since 1951”
