## What this mockup is

A working front-end prototype of the app Hy's could have, in three
connected sides of one system:

1. **Rider app** — book in taps instead of a phone call, pick a real
   vehicle with its fare up front, watch your chauffeur arrive live,
   and keep every trip and receipt in one place.
2. **Driver (chauffeur) app** — go online/offline, receive a trip
   request with a countdown, accept, drive the trip step by step
   (arrived → rider on board → drop-off), and see today's trips and
   earnings with a Friday payout.
3. **Control Center (dispatch)** — a live board for the West Haven
   office: KPIs, a fleet map with cars moving, the active-trips board
   with filters and a trip detail panel (message / reassign / call),
   an incoming-bookings queue with one-tap Assign, and the chauffeurs
   on shift.

The three sides stay in sync inside the demo: a rider booking lands
in the dispatch queue; accepting it on the driver side moves it to
the board as En route; completing it updates the driver's earnings.
Mode: Operate (app UI + dispatch dashboard). The phone is the product;
the control center is the pitch to the people who run the fleet.
