Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when the schedule allows. Custom designs are by appointment.

Is a deposit required?

Yes, for custom designs. It secures your date and goes toward the final price.

Can you cover up an old tattoo?

Cover-ups and repair are a specialty. Send clear photos with your consult request.

Do you do piercing?

Yes, professional body piercing is available.

How much will my tattoo cost?

It depends on size, detail and placement. You’ll get a quote at your consult.

What should I do before my session?

Eat beforehand, stay hydrated, and wear clothing that gives easy access to the area.
