Each feature below is demonstrated on this page with sample data. A real firm's system would be built for their trade, their booking form, and their own CRM behind it.

Smart booking questionnaire

Replaces the Contact Us page. Pet name, breed, temperament, service, vet, medications, vaccination records, and key/access notes arrive with the booking — no phone tag for the details.

Pet profiles

Temperament, medications, vet, and vaccination records in one place per client, so the next groomer, sitter, or walker never has to guess.

Photo/video updates mid-visit

The owner sees their pet during the groom, the stay, or the walk, in their own portal, instead of wondering how their pet is doing.

Booking board

Every booking on one board: booked → checked in → in progress → ready for pickup / walk complete.

Recurring bookings and packages

Weekly walks and grooming cycles booked as packages, with reminders when a regular goes quiet.

Walker/sitter route board

The day's walks and sits ordered on a route with staff assignment, so the team sees the whole day at a glance.

Client portal

Owners sign in with a magic link and see only their bookings: timeline, photo updates, package balance, and invoices.

Contracts and packages signed online

Service agreements and package plans sent and signed online, stored on the booking and in the portal.

Expenses and profit per booking

Supplies, labor, and sublet costs logged against each booking, so the business sees its real profit, not just its revenue.
